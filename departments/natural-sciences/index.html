❧ The study of the natural world

Natural Sciences

A district devoted to observing, documenting and understanding the extraordinary natural world. From the smallest living organisms to the forces that shape entire planets, every discovery brings us closer to understanding the realms.

Eight fields of discovery

The Departments

Each department preserves its own collections, conducts research and welcomes curious minds. Together, they study the life, matter and phenomena of countless worlds.

❧ DEPARTMENT 01

Department of Botany

Botany

Studies plants, trees, algae, fungi and other forms of plant life. Research includes medicinal species, magical flora, plant ecosystems and vegetation from unknown worlds.

Facilities: Greenhouses, experimental gardens, herbarium and seed bank.

♜ DEPARTMENT 02

Department of Zoology

Zoology

Investigates animals, fantastical creatures and their behaviour. Documents anatomy, reproduction, migration, feeding and relationships between species.

Facilities: Zoological archive, anatomy rooms, wildlife observatories and temporary care centres.

◇ DEPARTMENT 03

Department of Geology and Mineralogy

Geology and Mineralogy

Studies the composition, structure and history of planets, mountains, caves and minerals. Extraordinary crystals are catalogued and examined under safe conditions.

Facilities: Mineral collection, analysis laboratory, fossil archive and specimen chambers.

≈ DEPARTMENT 04

Department of Aquatic and Marine Sciences

Aquatic and Marine Sciences

Studies oceans, rivers, lakes, currents, underwater ecosystems and aquatic species. Works alongside marine communities and researchers exploring the depths.

Facilities: Research aquariums, underwater observatory, sample laboratory and habitats adapted to aquatic life.

☾ DEPARTMENT 05

Department of Astronomy and Cosmology

Astronomy and Cosmology

Investigates stars, moons, planets, solar systems and the structure of the universe. Compares astronomical phenomena across worlds and records extraordinary celestial events.

Facilities: Observatories, planetarium, star-chart archive and calculation rooms.

♧ DEPARTMENT 06

Department of Ecology and Environmental Studies

Ecology and Environmental Studies

Studies ecosystems, food webs, biodiversity, climates and the effects of different civilisations on their environments.

Facilities: Experimental biomes, observation stations and environmental change archive.

✧ DEPARTMENT 07

Department of Anatomy and Life Sciences

Anatomy and Life Sciences

Documents the biological and anatomical diversity of living beings. Investigates tissues, organs, life cycles and adaptations while respecting the autonomy and rights of conscious beings.

Facilities: Laboratories, anatomical collections, microscopy rooms and comparative archives.

☼ DEPARTMENT 08

Department of Meteorology and Natural Phenomena

Meteorology and Natural Phenomena

Records climates, storms, winds, earthquakes and unusual natural phenomena. Compares environmental conditions across different realms and worlds.

Facilities: Weather station, atmospheric simulation chambers and extreme phenomena archive.

Observe · Question · Understand

The natural world is never truly exhausted by study. Every answer opens the door to another question.
