About This Work

The Living Index is a systematic guide to the identification, naming and classification of plant life throughout the known realms. It introduces the principles used by botanical scholars to describe species, compare specimens, organise collections and record the extraordinary diversity of the world's flora.

This volume establishes the methods and terminology used throughout the botanical collections of the Grand Library. It also considers the difficulties posed by hybrid plants, unfamiliar ecosystems and species whose properties do not fit comfortably within established classifications.
