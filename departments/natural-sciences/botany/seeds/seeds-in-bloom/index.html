About This Volume

Seeds in Bloom records the seeds of flowering plants, from familiar garden flowers to wildflowers and unusual botanical specimens. Each species is treated as a chapter in a botanical volume, with its own observations, specimen notes and illustration plate.

Records will document seed form, dispersal, dormancy, germination and early plant development where known. Direct observations will be distinguished from established botanical information.

Opening Notes

Purpose & Record Method

Each species will receive an individual record with botanical description, seed morphology, flower-to-seed development, dormancy and germination, seedling development, approximate time record, propagation notes, field observations and an illustration plate.

Flowering plants vary widely. Some seeds require light, some darkness, some benefit from cold stratification, and others germinate readily when fresh. Treatments must be considered species by species.

Illustration plates are intentionally left blank until original drawings and specimen studies are added.

Field Register

Field Observation Register

For each specimen, record common and scientific names, family, source and location, seed dimensions and surface, dispersal method, collection date, storage conditions, dormancy treatment, germination conditions and dates of root and shoot emergence.

Where information is unavailable or uncertain, mark it as unrecorded rather than estimating a value.
