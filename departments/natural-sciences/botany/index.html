Welcome, Naturalist

Every Plant Has a Story

The Department of Botany studies the flora of every known realm: ordinary plants, ancient trees, medicinal herbs, endangered species and extraordinary forms of vegetation that challenge our understanding of nature.

Its scholars observe, document, experiment and preserve. Magic is investigated alongside natural processes, never used as a substitute for careful observation. Every discovery begins with a question.

“Every leaf is a record. Every seed, a possibility. Every forest, a library yet to be read.”
The Botanical Collections

The Twelve Sections

Twelve specialised branches work together to understand the extraordinary diversity of plant life throughout the realms.

01 ❧

Plant Taxonomy & Classification

Names, Families & Identification

Identifies, describes and classifies plant species, resolves conflicting names and investigates unknown specimens from distant regions and realms.

Signature Collection The Index of Species — the master catalogue of documented plants, their names, descriptions and illustrations.
02 ♧

Living Plant Collections

Gardens & Conservatories

Maintains living collections from rainforests, deserts, mountains, wetlands and unusual magical ecosystems in carefully controlled environments.

Signature Collection The Hundred Climates Conservatory — a monumental greenhouse divided into chambers with distinct environmental conditions.
03 ◉

Seeds & Propagation

Germination & Renewal

Studies germination, reproduction and propagation while preserving seeds and plant material for future research and the recovery of lost species.

Signature Collection The Seed Vault — rare seeds, ancient varieties and samples from plants of vanished realms.
04 ⚘

Medicinal Botany & Ethnobotany

Traditional Knowledge & Plant Uses

Documents plants used as food, remedies, dyes, fibres, materials and ritual objects, recording the communities and traditions that preserve this knowledge.

Signature Collection The Herbal Compendium — herbarium specimens, historical recipes, testimonies and records of traditional uses.
05 ✥

Toxic & Defensive Plants

Hazards, Toxins & Natural Defences

Investigates natural toxins, thorns, irritants, carnivorous plants and other defensive mechanisms. Dangerous specimens are handled under strict safety protocols.

Signature Collection The Garden of Untouchables — a secured research enclosure for plants requiring special handling.
06 ♜

Trees, Forests & Ancient Growth

Woodlands & Living History

Studies ancient trees, primeval forests, root systems and the long-term relationships between vegetation and its surrounding environment.

Signature Collection The Archive of Ancient Trees — growth rings, samples, illustrations and chronicles of legendary specimens.
07 ✧

Magical & Anomalous Botany

Enchanted Flora & Unexplained Phenomena

Investigates moon-responsive flowers, spell-storing plants, roots crossing dimensions and organisms that react to magical energy.

Signature Collection The Garden of Botanical Phenomena — a research garden where every anomaly is documented before explanations are proposed.
08 ⌖

Field Botany & Expeditions

Exploration & Field Research

Sends research teams into forests, ruins, islands, mountains and uncharted territories to document plants within their natural habitats.

Signature Collection The Expedition Archives — field journals, maps, drawings, specimens and accounts of journeys across the realms.
09 ✎

Botanical Illustration & Documentation

Art in the Service of Knowledge

Records plant species through drawings, engravings, watercolours, diagrams and detailed descriptions. Illustration preserves details that words may overlook.

Signature Collection The Illustrated Atlas of the Realms' Flora — an evolving visual record of botanical discoveries.
10 ◌

Plant Physiology & Adaptation

Growth, Function & Survival

Studies how plants grow, obtain energy, develop and respond to light, water, temperature and other environmental factors.

Signature Collection The Adaptation Laboratory — experimental chambers for investigating survival under unusual conditions.
11 ♧

Plant Conservation & Recovery

Protection & Restoration

Protects threatened species, investigates habitat loss and develops recovery projects. Records distinguish confirmed extinctions from missing or unverified species.

Signature Collection The Register of Lost Species — a record of last sightings, recovery efforts and unresolved botanical disappearances.
12 ❋

Plant Communication & Unusual Behaviour

Signals, Responses & Intelligence

Studies plant responses to stimuli, chemical signals and relationships with other organisms. In magical realms, it also investigates apparent recognition or intention.

Signature Collection The Notebooks of Unusual Plants — observations separating verified results, hypotheses and folklore.

A District Within the Library

Places of Botanical Study

The department occupies an interconnected network of archives, laboratories, gardens and conservatories.

The Green Archive

A quiet complex of herbarium rooms, specimen cabinets, seed collections, botanical books and illustration archives. Its catalogues connect centuries of research.

The Conservatories

A network of living environments for plants from different climates and realms, connected by glass corridors, observation galleries and enclosed gardens.

The Laboratories

Research rooms devoted to plant physiology, propagation, toxicity and unusual behaviour, with specialist facilities for controlled experiments.

The Outer Gardens

Open grounds for ancient trees, woodland plots, ecological studies and long-term conservation projects.

The Closed Garden

A restricted enclosure for hazardous, unstable or insufficiently understood specimens. Entry requires authorisation from the responsible researchers.

The Expedition Hall

The meeting point for field researchers, containing route archives, expedition journals, regional records and the equipment needed for botanical surveys.

Botanical research often collaborates with Medicine, Alchemy, Ecology, Astronomy and the Department of Arcane Arts. Discoveries are shared across the Library whenever knowledge from another discipline is required.
