Natural Sciences · Six fields of discovery

The Departments

Each department preserves its own collections, conducts research and welcomes curious minds. Together, they study the life, matter and phenomena of countless worlds.

DEPARTMENT 01

Department of Botany

Studies plants, trees, algae, fungi and other forms of plant life. Research includes medicinal species, magical flora, plant ecosystems and vegetation from unknown worlds.

Greenhouses · Experimental gardens · Herbarium · Seed bankExplore Botany → DEPARTMENT 02

Department of Zoology

Investigates animals, fantastical creatures and their behaviour. Documents anatomy, reproduction, migration, feeding and relationships between species.

Zoological archive · Anatomy rooms · Wildlife observatories · Care centresExplore Zoology → DEPARTMENT 03

Department of Geology and Mineralogy

Studies the composition, structure and history of planets, mountains, caves and minerals. Extraordinary crystals are catalogued and examined under safe conditions.

Mineral collection · Analysis laboratory · Fossil archive · Specimen chambersExplore Geology → DEPARTMENT 04

Department of Aquatic and Marine Sciences

Studies oceans, rivers, lakes, currents, underwater ecosystems and aquatic species. Works alongside marine communities and researchers exploring the depths.

Research aquariums · Underwater observatory · Sample laboratoryExplore Aquatic Sciences → DEPARTMENT 06

Department of Ecology, Meteorology and Natural Phenomena

Studies ecosystems, food webs, biodiversity, climates, atmospheric patterns and natural phenomena. It examines how weather, storms and environmental changes shape the living worlds of the realms.

Experimental biomes · Observation stations · Environmental archiveExplore Ecology → DEPARTMENT 07

Department of Anatomy and Life Sciences

Documents the biological and anatomical diversity of living beings. Investigates tissues, organs, life cycles and adaptations while respecting the autonomy and rights of conscious beings.

Laboratories · Anatomical collections · Microscopy rooms · Comparative archivesExplore Life Sciences →
