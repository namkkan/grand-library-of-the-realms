The West Gallery

The public passage from the Grand Central Hall towards Natural Sciences, botany and the Library's future living collections.

The Grand Library / The Grand Atrium / The West Gallery

The West Gallery is the western route out of the Central Hall. Its role is to guide visitors towards Natural Sciences while leaving room for the specialist spaces and greenhouse that will be developed later.

01 · Role

The Route to Living Knowledge

The West Gallery is a public passage and a major route into the Library's Natural Sciences district.

Explore details
Main destination

Natural Sciences

The gallery connects the Grand Central Hall with the Natural Sciences department. Its entrance should be easy to recognise without turning the passage itself into a full department.

  • Clear directional signs
  • A visible departmental threshold
  • Room for visitors to pause at the edges
Future expansion

Botanical Connections

Botany, seed collections and the future greenhouse can grow beyond this route. The gallery acts as their shared approach, while specialist spaces receive their own plans later.

  • Future greenhouse route to be decided
  • Potential links to botanical collections
  • No greenhouse placed on this plan yet
Planning principle: the West Gallery is the connector. Natural Sciences is the destination; the greenhouse and specialist rooms will be designed as separate spaces later.
