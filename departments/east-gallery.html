01 · Role

The Other Public Route

The East Gallery balances the West Gallery and provides a clear route towards other public wings of the Library.

Public circulation

Rooms Beyond the Hall

The gallery is a connector rather than a single collection. It leads visitors towards public rooms and future departments, with each destination receiving its own page when defined.

  • Open, legible route from the Central Hall
  • Signage at each junction
  • Space to wait without blocking circulation
Future destinations

Room to Grow

The exact rooms beyond this gallery remain intentionally flexible. The Grand Directory can list confirmed destinations without inventing a final floor plan too early.

  • Potential connections to public study spaces
  • Future wing entrances marked as they are established
  • Restricted routes clearly distinguished
Planning principle: the East Gallery should feel as carefully ordered as its western counterpart, but its eventual destinations will be decided independently.
