04 / At the entrance

Old Stones, Useful Magic

The entrance has acquired features over many generations. Some are remarkable; others are simply there because they work.

The main doors

The Doors of a Thousand Thresholds

Inscriptions from peoples who contributed to the Library survive across the doors. Visitors can activate an accessible translation when needed; the inscriptions do not change language by themselves.

Wayfinding

The Rose of Routes

A compass set into the floor points towards the principal districts rather than north. When a gallery closes, the wayfinding system indicates a safe alternative route.

Lighting

The Welcome Lanterns

The lanterns respond to ambient light and can be dimmed for visitors sensitive to bright illumination. They do not read emotions or thoughts.

Safety

Magic Is Not the Only Way

Physical evacuation signs and staff procedures remain available if an enchantment fails. Portals cannot open spontaneously inside the public entrance; authorised arrivals use a designated area.

One shared rule: no species, homeland, language, ancestry or social class receives preferential treatment. Entrances may close temporarily in an emergency, but safe alternative routes should be provided whenever possible.
