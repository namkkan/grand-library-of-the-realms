Welcome & Arrival

The threshold between the outside world and the city of knowledge. This is where the journey begins — before reception, catalogues or the great crossroads of the Atrium.

The Grand Library / The Grand Atrium / Welcome & Arrival
01 · Purpose

A Threshold, Not a Checkpoint

Welcome & Arrival is the first space a visitor encounters when entering the Grand Library of the Realms. It is not yet reception or the information centre: it is the transition between the outside world and the Library's immense shared world of knowledge.

Wonder

A Sense of Awe

The visitor understands that they have entered somewhere extraordinary: an institution shaped by centuries of expansion, exchange and discovery.

Welcome

A Place for Everyone

Every visitor should be able to feel welcome, regardless of species, homeland, language, social status or prior knowledge.

Orientation

A Clear Next Step

Even a first-time visitor should understand where they are and how to continue safely towards the Atrium and its public services.

Architecture

Centuries in One Entrance

Medieval doors, Gothic structures, Victorian details, ancient stonework, worked metal and discreet magic show that the entrance has been transformed over centuries rather than built all at once.

02 · The visitor journey

From the Outside World to the Library

Arrival should feel natural and understandable. Visitors can pause, look around and ask for help without being forced immediately into registration or a formal procedure.

First

The Entrance Threshold

Visitors cross the monumental doors and enter a space that marks the transition into the Library. Physical signs identify the public entrance and accessible routes.

Then

The Arrival Area

Visitors can stop, meet companions, use the facilities, consult signs or ask an Arrival Steward for initial directions.

Next

Orientation

Signs and staff help visitors identify the Grand Atrium, the public directory and the route appropriate to their destination.

Finally

Into the Grand Atrium

Visitors who need more detailed information continue to the Atrium, where the main directory and specialist visitor services are located.

03 · Personnel

The Arrival Team

These are institutional roles, not species-based jobs. Staff may come from any people or realm, and specialisation depends on training and experience.

Position Function
Welcome Stewards Welcome arriving visitors and offer initial help or directions.
Arrival Attendants Manage visitor flow, keep routes clear and help prevent crowding.
Accessibility Guides Assist visitors with different physical, sensory, communication or mobility needs.
Multilingual Hosts Help in commonly used languages and arrange interpreters when needed.
Threshold Wardens Monitor entrance safety and respond to incidents while treating ordinary visitors respectfully.
Equal opportunity: a siren might specialise in supporting aquatic visitors, a kenku might know many languages, and a human might be an accessibility expert. These are examples of possible specialisations, not rules about what any people can do.
04 · Facilities

Facilities & Immediate Assistance

The threshold should help people pause, recover from their journey and prepare to enter the Library without forcing them to disclose unnecessary personal information.

Rest & Comfort

Waiting Areas

  • Benches and seats in different sizes and heights
  • Meeting points for companions and groups
  • Water fountains and refill points
  • Coatroom and space for wet clothing or travel equipment
Access & Communication

Clear Ways Through

  • Visual, tactile, audible and multilingual signs
  • Step-free entrances and routes for different mobility needs
  • Space to pause without blocking the main flow
  • Non-magical signs that remain usable if enchantments fail
Immediate Support

Help When Needed

  • A small first-aid and immediate-assistance point
  • Staff able to call medical or specialist help
  • Procedures for lost visitors and separated groups
Special Arrivals

Authorised Portals

  • A designated arrival area for visitors using authorised portals
  • Portals may not open spontaneously inside the public entrance
  • Portal arrivals follow the same public routes and equal-access rules
05 · Fantastical features

Magic with Clear Limits

The entrance uses magic to support welcome and orientation, never to replace consent, accessibility or human assistance.

The Main Entrance

The Doors of a Thousand Thresholds

The great doors preserve inscriptions from peoples who have contributed to the Library across its history. The inscriptions do not magically change language; visitors can activate an accessible translation when they need one.

Wayfinding

The Rose of Routes

A monumental compass set into the floor points towards the Library's principal districts rather than north. If a gallery is closed, the wayfinding system indicates a safe alternative.

Lighting

The Welcome Lanterns

The lanterns adjust to ambient light and can be dimmed for visitors sensitive to bright illumination. They do not read emotions or thoughts and are not a surveillance system.

Reliability

Ordinary Systems Remain

Physical evacuation signs and staff procedures remain available if magic fails. No visitor should be left without a safe route because an enchantment is unavailable.

06 · Rules & limits

What This Space Does — and Does Not Do

Welcome & Arrival is public, but its role is deliberately limited so visitors know where to go next.

Public Access

Open and Welcoming

The space is open during general public hours. Entering the Library does not automatically require registration, and staff should not collect unnecessary personal information.

Safe Arrival

Controlled Portal Access

Spontaneous portals are not permitted to open directly into the entrance. Service doors and restricted-area access points remain outside public routes.

Fair Treatment

No Preferred Peoples

No species, realm, language, ancestry or social class receives preferential treatment. Access adaptations are part of ordinary service.

Emergencies

Keep a Way Through

Staff may temporarily close an entrance during an emergency, but should provide alternative routes whenever it is safe and possible.

Scope: registration for specific collections, specialist consultation and permission to enter restricted areas are handled by the relevant services elsewhere in the Library. Welcome & Arrival gets people safely inside and helps them find their next destination.
