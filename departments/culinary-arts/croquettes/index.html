The Golden Croquette Base

About 20 croquettes · Intermediate · 30 min + 4 hours chilling + frying

Ingredients

  • 60 g butter
  • 65 g plain flour
  • 500 ml whole milk
  • Salt, pepper and nutmeg to taste
  • 60 g flour for coating
  • 2 beaten eggs
  • 120 g breadcrumbs
  • Neutral frying oil

Preparation

  1. Melt butter, stir in flour and cook for 2 minutes.
  2. Gradually whisk in warm milk. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring, until very thick and pulling away from the pan, about 10–15 minutes.
  3. Add the chosen filling as directed. Season to taste.
  4. Spread in a shallow container, cool promptly and refrigerate within two hours. Chill at least four hours.
  5. Shape into about 20 small logs. Coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs.
  6. Fry in small batches at 175°C until golden and the centre reaches 74°C. Drain and allow to cool briefly before eating.

Bai's Notes

Patience, my friends! Cold dough is our best friend. And please do not bite straight into one from the fryer — even a tiger has limits!

Refrigerate cooked leftovers within two hours and use within 3–4 days; reheat to 74°C.
