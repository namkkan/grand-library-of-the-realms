Traditional Italian Tiramisu

6 servings · Intermediate · 30 minutes + 6 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 250 g mascarpone
  • 3 pasteurised eggs
  • 70 g sugar
  • 200 g ladyfingers
  • 250 ml strong coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Separate the eggs. Whisk yolks and sugar until pale, then mix gently with mascarpone.
  2. Whisk whites to stiff peaks and fold into the cream.
  3. Briefly dip the ladyfingers into the cooled coffee.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream twice in a dish.
  5. Cover and chill at 4°C or colder for at least 6 hours. Dust with cocoa before serving.

Bai's Notes

Coffee, cocoa and clouds of mascarpone! The hardest part is waiting for it to chill.

Dust with cocoa just before serving. Do not soak the biscuits. Use pasteurised eggs as the cream is uncooked. Keep refrigerated and consume within two days.

Estimated energy: 390 kcal per serving, excluding optional chocolate decoration.

Whisky & Chocolate Tiramisu

6 servings · Intermediate · 30 minutes + 6 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 250 g mascarpone
  • 3 pasteurised eggs
  • 70 g sugar
  • 200 g ladyfingers
  • 250 ml strong coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 40 ml whisky
  • Vanilla to taste
  • Dark and white chocolate for decoration

Preparation

  1. Separate the eggs. Whisk yolks and sugar until pale, then mix gently with mascarpone.
  2. Whisk whites to stiff peaks and fold into the cream.
  3. Briefly dip the ladyfingers into the cooled coffee mixed with whisky.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream twice in a dish.
  5. Cover and chill at 4°C or colder for at least 6 hours. Dust with cocoa before serving.

Bai's Notes

Chocolate curls on top? Make a few extra. For quality control, obviously!

Add vanilla to the cream. Finish with dark and white chocolate shavings or curls. The whisky remains alcoholic. Do not soak the biscuits. Use pasteurised eggs as the cream is uncooked. Keep refrigerated and consume within two days.

Estimated energy: 410 kcal per serving, excluding optional chocolate decoration.

Traditional Egg Flan

6 servings · Easy · 20 min + 45–60 min baking + 4 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g sugar and 1 tbsp water for caramel

Preparation

  1. Heat the caramel sugar and water until amber. Carefully pour into six heatproof ramekins.
  2. Whisk eggs with sugar gently, then stir in milk. Strain into the ramekins.
  3. Place in a roasting pan and add hot water halfway up the sides. Bake at 160°C for 45–60 minutes, until set with a slight wobble and the centre reaches 71°C.
  4. Cool briefly, refrigerate within two hours, chill for at least four hours and unmould.

Bai's Notes

That little caramel waterfall when you turn it out makes me ridiculously happy.

Avoid whipping air into the custard for a smooth texture. Hot caramel can cause severe burns.

Approximately 200 kcal per serving.

Cream Cheese Flan

6 servings · Easy · 20 min + 50–65 min baking + 4 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 200 g cream cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 400 ml whole milk
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g sugar and 1 tbsp water for caramel

Preparation

  1. Prepare amber caramel and carefully distribute into heatproof ramekins.
  2. Mix softened cream cheese with sugar, then gradually incorporate eggs and milk without aerating. Strain if needed.
  3. Bake in a hot-water bath at 160°C for 50–65 minutes, until set with a slight wobble and the centre reaches 71°C.
  4. Cool briefly, refrigerate within two hours, chill at least four hours and unmould.

Bai's Notes

A flan AND cream cheese? Someone bring me a spoon!

Bring cream cheese to room temperature briefly before mixing to prevent lumps.

Approximately 300 kcal per serving, depending on cheese.

Cherry & Walnut Pudding

8 servings · Family recipe · Provisional quantities

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 150 g ladyfingers
  • 80 g walnuts
  • 100 g drained cherries in syrup
  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g sugar and 1 tbsp water for caramel

Preparation

  1. Prepare amber caramel and carefully coat the bottom of a heatproof mould.
  2. Layer ladyfingers, walnuts and cherries.
  3. Gently mix milk, eggs and sugar and pour over the layers. Allow to soak for 10 minutes.
  4. Bake in a hot-water bath at 160°C for 50–65 minutes, until set and the centre reaches 71°C.
  5. Cool briefly, refrigerate within two hours and chill for at least four hours before unmoulding.

Bai's Notes

Family recipes are treasures. I cannot wait to hear how Grandmother makes this one!

The family ingredients are confirmed. These quantities and detailed cooking instructions are a provisional reconstruction, to be checked with the grandmother. Approx. 280 kcal per serving.

Fried Anise Rings

20 rings · Family recipe · Provisional quantities

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 350–400 g wheat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 g sugar
  • 50 ml anise liqueur
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 10 g baking powder
  • 60 ml mild oil for dough
  • Oil for frying
  • Sugar for coating

Preparation

  1. Whisk eggs and sugar, then mix in anise liqueur, lemon zest and oil.
  2. Gradually incorporate flour and baking powder until a soft, shapeable dough forms. Rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Shape into small rings with lightly oiled hands.
  4. Fry in batches at 165–170°C until golden on both sides and cooked through.
  5. Drain and coat with sugar while still warm.

Bai's Notes

Golden little rings dusted with sugar! I would happily make a mountain of them.

The family ingredients are confirmed. These quantities and detailed cooking instructions are a provisional reconstruction, to be checked with the grandmother. Approx. 150 kcal per ring, depending on oil absorption.

Almond & Lemon Cake

8 servings · Easy · 15 min preparation + 30–40 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 250 g ground almonds
  • 250 g sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • Icing sugar for dusting
  • Butter for greasing the tin

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Grease and line a 22–24 cm round tin.
  2. Mix the eggs and sugar until combined. Stir in ground almonds, lemon zest and optional cinnamon.
  3. Pour into the tin and bake for 30–40 minutes until golden and set in the centre.
  4. Cool completely, unmould and dust with icing sugar.

Bai's Notes

Almonds and lemon smell like sunshine in my kitchen.

A traditional Galician-style almond cake without wheat flour in the batter. No decorative stencil is required. Estimated energy: 360 kcal per serving, excluding extra dusting sugar.

Fluffy Yogurt Sponge Cake

10 servings · Easy · 20 min preparation + 35–45 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 125 g plain yogurt
  • 3 eggs, separated
  • 180 g sugar
  • 225 g plain wheat flour
  • 100 ml mild vegetable oil
  • 12 g baking powder
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C (conventional heat). Line a 22 cm round cake tin.
  2. Whisk the yolks with 120 g sugar until pale. Mix in yogurt and oil.
  3. Sift flour and baking powder together and incorporate gently.
  4. In a clean bowl, whisk the egg whites with the salt, gradually adding the remaining 60 g sugar until glossy firm peaks form.
  5. Fold one third of the whites into the batter to loosen it, then gently fold in the rest with a spatula.
  6. Transfer immediately to the tin and bake for 35–45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Avoid opening the oven during the first 30 minutes.
  7. Cool for 10 minutes in the tin, then transfer to a rack.

Bai's Notes

Look at that fluffy sponge! Fold gently; we want to keep every little cloud of air.

This is a foundation sponge for layered cakes, fillings and flavour variations. Add lemon zest, vanilla or orange zest if desired. Fold carefully to preserve the air in the whipped whites. Estimated energy: 290 kcal per serving.

Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

18 cookies · Easy · 20 min preparation + 30 min chilling + 10–12 min baking per batch

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 115 g softened unsalted butter
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 60 g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 190 g plain wheat flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 150 g chocolate chips

Preparation

  1. Cream butter and both sugars until fluffy.
  2. Mix in egg and vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, baking soda and salt, then mix into the wet ingredients just until combined.
  4. Fold in chocolate chips and refrigerate the dough for 30 minutes.
  5. Preheat oven to 175°C. Divide into 18 balls and space apart on lined baking trays.
  6. Bake for 10–12 minutes until edges are golden and centres are set but soft.
  7. Leave on the tray for 5 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.

Bai's Notes

Warm cookies and melting chocolate. Is there a better kitchen perfume?

Brown sugar helps keep the centre chewy, while white sugar encourages crisp edges. Reserve a few chocolate chips to press into the dough balls before baking. Estimated energy: 175 kcal per cookie.

Classic Cinnamon Rolls

12 rolls · Intermediate · 30 min preparation + 90–150 min rising + 22–28 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

Dough

  • 450 g strong wheat flour
  • 240 ml lukewarm milk
  • 7 g instant yeast
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g softened unsalted butter
  • 5 g salt

Filling

  • 60 g softened butter
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 12 g ground cinnamon

Cream cheese frosting

  • 100 g cream cheese
  • 30 g softened butter
  • 60 g icing sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, milk, egg and salt. Knead, gradually incorporating the butter, until smooth and elastic, about 10–15 minutes.
  2. Cover and leave to rise until doubled, about 60–90 minutes.
  3. Roll into a 40 × 30 cm rectangle. Spread with softened butter and sprinkle with mixed brown sugar and cinnamon.
  4. Roll tightly from the long edge and cut into 12 pieces. Arrange in a greased baking dish.
  5. Cover and let rise for 30–60 minutes until puffy.
  6. Bake at 180°C conventional heat for 22–28 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  7. Beat frosting ingredients until smooth and spread over warm, not hot, rolls.

Bai's Notes

Cinnamon spirals! I could watch them rise all afternoon.

Use lukewarm rather than hot milk. Judge proofing by dough volume rather than the clock. Avoid adding excessive flour to keep the rolls soft. Refrigerate leftovers with cream cheese frosting. Estimated energy: 370 kcal per roll.

Classic Apple Pie

8 servings · Intermediate · 35 min + 60 min chilling + 50–60 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 350 g plain wheat flour
  • 225 g cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 60–90 ml ice water
  • 900 g apples, peeled and cored
  • 100 g sugar
  • 20 g cornflour
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg for brushing

Preparation

  1. Rub cold butter into flour and salt until coarse crumbs form. Add ice water gradually until dough holds together. Divide into two discs, wrap and chill for 60 minutes.
  2. Slice apples and toss with sugar, cornflour, lemon juice and cinnamon.
  3. Roll one disc into a 23 cm pie dish. Add filling, cover with the second rolled disc, seal edges and cut steam vents.
  4. Brush with beaten egg. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes, then at 180°C for 35–45 minutes until golden and filling bubbles.
  5. Cool for at least two hours before slicing.

Bai's Notes

A golden crust hiding soft apples — now THAT is a lovely surprise.

Choose firm, slightly tart apples. A lattice top is optional. Estimated energy: 480 kcal per serving.

Baked Apples

4 servings · Easy · 10 min + 30–40 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 4 medium apples
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100 ml water
  • 20 g butter (optional)

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Wash apples and remove cores without cutting through the bases.
  2. Place in a baking dish. Fill centres with sugar and cinnamon, adding a little butter if desired.
  3. Pour water around the apples and bake for 30–40 minutes until tender but holding their shape.
  4. Serve warm with the juices from the dish.

Bai's Notes

The humble apple deserves applause for this one!

Use apples that hold their shape during baking. Sugar and butter can be omitted for a simpler version. Estimated energy: 140 kcal per serving with butter.

Baked Pears

4 servings · Easy · 10 min preparation + 25–35 min baking

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 4 firm ripe pears
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 100 ml water
  • 20 g butter (optional)

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Wash pears, halve lengthwise and scoop out the cores.
  3. Arrange cut side up in a baking dish, sprinkle with sugar and cinnamon and add optional butter.
  4. Pour water around the pears and bake for 25–35 minutes until tender.
  5. Serve warm with the cooking juices.

Bai's Notes

Soft pears and cinnamon-scented juices. Save me a little, please!

Choose firm ripe pears so they retain their shape. Sugar and butter may be omitted. Estimated energy: 165 kcal per serving with butter.
