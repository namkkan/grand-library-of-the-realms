Traditional Italian Tiramisu

6 servings · Intermediate · 30 minutes + 6 hours chilling · Not confirmed as tested

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 250 g mascarpone
  • 3 pasteurised eggs
  • 70 g sugar
  • 200 g ladyfingers
  • 250 ml strong coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Separate the eggs. Whisk yolks and sugar until pale, then mix gently with mascarpone.
  2. Whisk whites to stiff peaks and fold into the cream.
  3. Briefly dip the ladyfingers into the cooled coffee.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream twice in a dish.
  5. Cover and chill at 4°C or colder for at least 6 hours. Dust with cocoa before serving.

Bai's Notes

Dust with cocoa just before serving. Do not soak the biscuits. Use pasteurised eggs as the cream is uncooked. Keep refrigerated and consume within two days.

Estimated energy: 390 kcal per serving, excluding optional chocolate decoration.

Recipe 002 · Head Chef Bai

Whisky & Chocolate Tiramisu

6 servings · Intermediate · 30 minutes + 6 hours chilling · Not confirmed as tested

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 250 g mascarpone
  • 3 pasteurised eggs
  • 70 g sugar
  • 200 g ladyfingers
  • 250 ml strong coffee, cooled
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 40 ml whisky
  • Vanilla to taste
  • Dark and white chocolate for decoration

Preparation

  1. Separate the eggs. Whisk yolks and sugar until pale, then mix gently with mascarpone.
  2. Whisk whites to stiff peaks and fold into the cream.
  3. Briefly dip the ladyfingers into the cooled coffee mixed with whisky.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream twice in a dish.
  5. Cover and chill at 4°C or colder for at least 6 hours. Dust with cocoa before serving.

Bai's Notes

Add vanilla to the cream. Finish with dark and white chocolate shavings or curls. The whisky remains alcoholic. Do not soak the biscuits. Use pasteurised eggs as the cream is uncooked. Keep refrigerated and consume within two days.

Estimated energy: 410 kcal per serving, excluding optional chocolate decoration.

Recipe 003 · Head Chef Bai

Traditional Egg Flan

6 servings · Easy · 20 min + 45–60 min baking + 4 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g sugar and 1 tbsp water for caramel

Preparation

  1. Heat the caramel sugar and water until amber. Carefully pour into six heatproof ramekins.
  2. Whisk eggs with sugar gently, then stir in milk. Strain into the ramekins.
  3. Place in a roasting pan and add hot water halfway up the sides. Bake at 160°C for 45–60 minutes, until set with a slight wobble and the centre reaches 71°C.
  4. Cool briefly, refrigerate within two hours, chill for at least four hours and unmould.

Bai's Notes

Avoid whipping air into the custard for a smooth texture. Hot caramel can cause severe burns.

Approximately 200 kcal per serving.

Cream Cheese Flan

6 servings · Easy · 20 min + 50–65 min baking + 4 hours chilling

Illustration to come

Ingredients

  • 200 g cream cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • 400 ml whole milk
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g sugar and 1 tbsp water for caramel

Preparation

  1. Prepare amber caramel and carefully distribute into heatproof ramekins.
  2. Mix softened cream cheese with sugar, then gradually incorporate eggs and milk without aerating. Strain if needed.
  3. Bake in a hot-water bath at 160°C for 50–65 minutes, until set with a slight wobble and the centre reaches 71°C.
  4. Cool briefly, refrigerate within two hours, chill at least four hours and unmould.

Bai's Notes

Bring cream cheese to room temperature briefly before mixing to prevent lumps.

Approximately 300 kcal per serving, depending on cheese.

Cherry & Walnut Pudding

Family recipe · Method confirmed · Quantities pending

Illustration to come

Ingredients confirmed so far

  • Ladyfingers (savoiardi)
  • Walnuts
  • Cherries in syrup
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Caramel for the base

Preparation

  1. Coat the bottom of the mould with caramel.
  2. Arrange the ladyfingers, walnuts and cherries in syrup in layers.
  3. Mix the milk, eggs and sugar and pour over the layers.
  4. Cook in a bain-marie (water bath).
