I — Candied Fruits

Candied and glacé fruits.

01 — Candied Orange

Ingredients: 500 g prepared fruit, 500 g sugar, 500 ml water.

  1. Slice washed oranges into 5 mm rounds. Blanch twice in fresh boiling water for 3 minutes each, then drain.
  2. Heat sugar and water until dissolved. Add fruit and simmer gently for 45–60 minutes, checking tenderness and translucency; avoid breaking the fruit.
  3. Cool promptly in syrup and refrigerate within two hours. Rest overnight in the refrigerator.
  4. The next day, drain the syrup into a saucepan, bring to a boil and reduce slightly, then return the fruit and simmer gently for 5 minutes. Cool promptly and refrigerate again.
  5. Drain on a rack in the refrigerator. For an optional crystallised finish, coat drained pieces in granulated sugar.

Bai's Notes: This is a simplified home-candied version, not a validated shelf-stable confection. Keep refrigerated at 4°C or below and use within 3–4 days after the final cooking, or freeze. Optional food colouring may be used for melon or pumpkin. Quantities and timings are provisional; not confirmed as tested.

II — Jams & Marmalades

Fruit jams, marmalades and conserves.

01 — Apple Jam

Ingredients: 500 g prepared fruit, 350 g sugar, 1 tbsp lemon juice.

  1. Peel, core and dice apples; add 80 ml water.
  2. Combine fruit, sugar and lemon juice in a saucepan. Heat gently until sugar dissolves.
  3. Simmer uncovered, stirring often, for 15–40 minutes or until thickened; test on a chilled plate. For pineapple and coconut, follow the coconut timing above.
  4. Transfer to clean heatproof containers, cool promptly and refrigerate within two hours.

Bai's Notes: Refrigerate at 4°C or below and use within 7 days, or freeze in suitable containers. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe. Quantities are provisional and not confirmed as tested.

III — Fruits in Syrup

Fruit prepared in sweet syrup.

01 — Pineapple in Syrup

Ingredients: 500 g prepared fruit, 500 ml water, 200 g sugar, 1 tbsp lemon juice.

  1. Peel, core and slice.
  2. Bring water, sugar and lemon juice to a gentle boil until sugar dissolves.
  3. Add fruit and simmer gently for 10–15 minutes, until tender but holding its shape.
  4. Transfer fruit and syrup to clean heatproof containers, cool promptly and refrigerate within two hours.

Bai's Notes: Keep fruit covered in syrup, refrigerate at 4°C or below and consume within 3–4 days. Not a shelf-stable canning recipe. Not confirmed as tested.
