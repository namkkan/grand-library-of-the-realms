Record 001 · Introduction

Overview

Sapphiron is a kenku wizard and independent alchemist who lives near the city surrounding the Grand Library of the Realms. A quiet, curious scholar, he devotes much of his time to the study of magic, transmutation and the natural properties of unusual materials.

Although he is a familiar presence in the Library's reading rooms and collections, he is not a member of its official staff. He maintains his independence, pursuing his own investigations and sharing selected findings with the institution.

"Knowledge is worth pursuing, even when the answer leads to another question."

Appearance

Sapphiron has dark crow-like plumage whose subtle iridescence changes with the light. His eyes are blue, a colour that seems to have found its way into almost every corner of his life. He wears no mask and has no hair; his natural features remain unobscured.

Among his most recognisable possessions is a sapphire pendant worn close to his chest. It is an object he never removes.

Temperament

Reserved and introspective, Sapphiron often prefers the company of books, experiments and quiet places. His shyness should not be mistaken for indifference: curiosity draws him towards unfamiliar knowledge, and he can be unexpectedly kind to those who earn his trust.

He avoids physical confrontation whenever possible and favours observation, preparation and magic over violence. Despite his solitary habits, he remains deeply interested in the world beyond his cabin.

Institutional Association

A Reader, Not an Employee

Sapphiron holds a reader's registration with the Grand Library of the Realms. He visits to consult its collections, pursue research and study subjects that cannot easily be explored in isolation.

From time to time, he contributes observations, experimental results and discoveries that may be useful to the Library's scholars. These contributions are received in his capacity as an independent collaborator, not as an official librarian or member of staff.

Registry Note

Institutional status: external reader and occasional contributor. No official appointment is recorded.

Fields of Study

Research & Alchemy

Sapphiron's principal interests are alchemy, transmutation, magical theory and the properties of rare or unusual materials. His work often begins with the gathering and examination of ingredients collected in the surrounding forest.

He records his observations carefully and approaches unfamiliar phenomena with patience. Not every experiment yields a useful result, but even an unsuccessful attempt may reveal something worth preserving.

Known Fields

  • Alchemy and experimental mixtures
  • Transmutation magic
  • Rare botanical and mineral materials
  • Magical phenomena and unexplained properties
  • Independent scholarly research

His current investigations remain independent of any formal Library department.

Place of Residence

The Cabin in the Ancient Forest

Beyond the city, among the trees of an ancient forest, Sapphiron lives in a modest wooden cabin. It is a quiet, practical place, built around the needs of someone who spends his days reading, gathering materials and experimenting.

Inside, shelves of books share the space with alchemical equipment, carefully stored ingredients and handwritten notes. Deep blue accents appear throughout the cabin: a blue lamp, small personal objects and feathers that catch the light.

The cabin is not a grand tower or a wizard's fortress. It is a home: private, familiar and deliberately removed from the constant activity of the city.

Personal Object · Unresolved Record

The Sapphire Pendant

Sapphiron wears a sapphire pendant that he never removes. Its deep blue stone is one of the few personal possessions that appears to remain constantly with him.

The pendant is connected to a promise. The circumstances surrounding that promise, its intended meaning and the identity of anyone else involved have not been established in this public record.

Unresolved

The meaning of the promise remains undisclosed. No further conclusion should be drawn from the available information.

For now, the pendant remains a personal matter rather than a subject of the Library's official investigations.
