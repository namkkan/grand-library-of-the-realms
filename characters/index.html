Welcome to the Archive

Stories Worth Preserving

The Grand Library stands among many peoples, cultures and histories. Some individuals are closely connected to the institution; others pass through its doors only occasionally, bringing with them discoveries, questions and stories from beyond its walls.

This archive gathers the personal records of those who inhabit the world of the Realms. Each entry offers a glimpse into an individual life, while acknowledging that no written record can contain the whole of a person.
