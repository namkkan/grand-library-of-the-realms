Hyssop

The Arrival Guide · Grand Library of the Realms

A small, mushroom-like being who welcomes newcomers to the Grand Library. Hyssop knows its public routes, remembers curious details that others overlook, and can turn even the simplest question into a long and enthusiastic conversation.

Species Mycelium-born
Age Approximately 100–300 years
Height 115–130 cm
Gender Androgynous
Occupation Arrival Guide

01

Appearance

Hyssop is short and round, with an unmistakably asymmetrical silhouette. His irregular cap tilts to one side, framing large, round, expressive eyes. Delicate gills sit beneath the cap, while small mushrooms and other organic growths emerge unevenly across his body. He has no hair, and his physical form remains stable throughout his long life.

He dresses in a somewhat dishevelled, Victorian-inspired outfit: usually a burgundy coat, a cream waistcoat, antique brass buttons and a monocle suspended from a fine chain. His many pockets are full of notes, labels and miscellaneous objects he has collected along the way.

Signature detail: a faint cloud of spores that sometimes follows him when he is especially pleased.

02

A Curious Nature

Lost in a Book

When Hyssop becomes absorbed in a book, he may release a tiny cloud of spores without noticing. The same thing happens when he feels he has genuinely helped someone. The pale, almost golden dust hangs in the air for a moment before settling.

One Question Becomes Ten

Hyssop remembers obscure facts and gets distracted by fascinating details. A simple request for directions can become an impromptu lecture about a forgotten reading room, an unusual staircase or a visitor from centuries ago.

His spores are involuntary, the fungal equivalent of blushing. Hyssop cannot quite hide his delight when a book catches his attention or when a newcomer leaves feeling less lost.

03

Discovery in the Library

Hyssop was discovered in a damp basement near the Department of Botany. He had awakened alone and was already moving books around when the first staff members found him. No one could quite work out what he was doing, but he seemed to know exactly where each volume belonged—or at least had a very enthusiastic explanation for every change.

Before long, he began interrupting formal tours to add forgotten details and correct historical inaccuracies. Visitors started asking for him by name. The Library eventually recognised what they already knew: nobody could make a newcomer feel more welcome.

04

The Arrival Guide

Hyssop works in Welcome & Arrival, helping visitors orient themselves, understand the public areas and find the right entrance, service or route. He is especially good with first-time visitors, who can find the Library's scale overwhelming.

For Hyssop, guiding is more than a job. It is a chance to share the wonder of the Library with someone who has never experienced it before. He may take a little longer than expected, but he wants every visitor to leave with a sense that they belong here.

05

Among the Mycelium-born

A People Yet to Be Documented

Hyssop belongs to the Mycelium-born, a people whose biology, varieties, communities and traditions will be explored in a dedicated species archive. His spore release is one expression of his own nature; it should not be assumed that every Mycelium-born behaves in the same way.

“Welcome, welcome! Now, where have you travelled from? Oh, never mind, tell me on the way!”

— Hyssop, The Arrival Guide
