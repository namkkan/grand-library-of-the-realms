Overview

Hyssop is a curious, mushroom-like being who welcomes newcomers to the Grand Library. He knows its public routes, remembers obscure details, and can turn even the simplest question into a long and enthusiastic conversation.

He wants visitors to feel that they belong here. His explanations sometimes wander, but his enthusiasm is genuine, and he rarely lets a newcomer leave without feeling a little less lost.

“A good guide doesn't just show you where to go. They make you want to see what's there.”

Appearance

Hyssop is short and round, with an unmistakably asymmetrical silhouette. His irregular cap tilts to one side, framing large, round, expressive eyes. Delicate gills sit beneath the cap, while small mushrooms and organic growths emerge unevenly across his body. He has no hair, and his physical form remains stable throughout his long life.

He dresses in a somewhat dishevelled, Victorian-inspired outfit: usually a burgundy coat, a cream waistcoat, antique brass buttons and a monocle suspended from a fine chain. His many pockets are full of notes, labels and miscellaneous objects he has collected along the way.

Signature Detail

When Hyssop becomes absorbed in a book or feels he has genuinely helped someone, he releases a tiny cloud of pale, almost golden spores. It is involuntary—the fungal equivalent of blushing—and he rarely notices it himself.

Personality

A Curious Nature

Hyssop remembers obscure facts and gets distracted by fascinating details. A simple request for directions can become an impromptu lecture about a forgotten reading room, an unusual staircase or a visitor from centuries ago.

He is most animated when a book catches his attention or when a newcomer leaves feeling less lost. Those are the moments when the little clouds of spores tend to appear.

Background

Discovery in the Library

Hyssop was discovered in a damp basement near the Department of Botany. He had awakened alone and was already moving books around when the first staff members found him. No one could quite work out what he was doing, but he seemed to know exactly where each volume belonged—or at least had a very enthusiastic explanation for every change.

Before long, he began interrupting formal tours to add forgotten details and correct historical inaccuracies. Visitors started asking for him by name. The Library eventually recognised what they already knew: nobody could make a newcomer feel more welcome.

Library Role

Hyssop's Role

Hyssop works in Welcome & Arrival, helping visitors orient themselves, understand the public areas and find the right entrance, service or route. He is especially good with first-time visitors, who can find the Library's scale overwhelming.

For Hyssop, guiding is more than a job. It is a chance to share the wonder of the Library with someone who has never experienced it before. He may take a little longer than expected, but he wants every visitor to leave with a sense that they belong here.
