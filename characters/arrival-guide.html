The Arrival Guide

Welcome & Arrival · District of Knowledge

An eccentric, endlessly curious host who welcomes newcomers to the Grand Library of the Realms. They know its entrances and public routes remarkably well, although a simple question can easily turn into a conversation that lasts half an hour.

Species Mycelium-born
Presentation Androgynous
Occupation Arrival Guide
Disposition Curious & welcoming

01

Appearance

The Arrival Guide has a distinctly mushroom-like appearance, paired with the carefully assembled elegance of Victorian clothing. Their outfit combines a burgundy coat, a cream waistcoat and an antique monocle attached to a delicate brass chain.

Their appearance is unusual even by the standards of a library populated by many different peoples. They wear their eccentricity with confidence and seem entirely at home among the Library's endless corridors.

Signature item: an antique monocle with a delicate brass chain.

02

Personality & Habits

Always Curious

Every traveller is a potential conversation. The Arrival Guide loves discovering where visitors have come from, what they are looking for and what brought them to the Grand Library in the first place.

Prone to Rambling

Directions are rarely just directions. A question about the nearest reading room might lead to a story about an unusual staircase, an unexpected detour or a fascinating visitor from years ago.

03

Role in the Library

The Arrival Guide works in Welcome & Arrival, within the District of Knowledge. Their task is to help visitors orient themselves, understand the public areas and find the right entrance, service or route for their needs.

They are particularly useful to first-time visitors, who can find the Library's scale overwhelming. The Arrival Guide knows the public routes remarkably well and can usually point travellers in the right direction, even if the explanation takes considerably longer than expected.

04

Among the Mycelium-born

A People Yet to Be Documented

The Arrival Guide belongs to the Mycelium-born, a people whose biology, varieties, communities and traditions will be explored in a dedicated species archive. This character's personality and habits are their own; they do not necessarily represent the entire species.

“Welcome, welcome! Now, where have you travelled from? Oh, never mind, tell me on the way!”
